Orientation

What Is This Competition About?

TREC RAG asks a system to do two hard things together: find useful evidence for a complex information need, then write a cited answer that is actually supported by that evidence.

Core task

Turn broad, realistic questions into grounded answers with retrievable evidence and visible attribution.

Why it is hard

The system must balance recall, precision, synthesis, and citation faithfulness without drifting from the sources.

Newcomer lens

Think of the track as retrieval plus answer writing, evaluated as one connected evidence pipeline.

ClimbMix

What Is ClimbMix?

ClimbMix is the 2026 evidence collection. For newcomers, the important idea is that retrieval and citations point to official ClimbMix document IDs, even if a system uses smaller chunks internally.

Broad corpus

It is a broad web-style text collection rather than a narrow domain dataset.

Official IDs

The submitted retrieval rows and RAG references should use ClimbMix document IDs.

System impact

Chunking, reranking, and evidence selection can happen internally, but final citations need to remain traceable.

2025 Context

What Happened In 2025?

The 2025 context separates track setup, common failure modes, participant choices, and the practical lessons that carry into 2026.

  • Task framing The 2025 task coupled retrieval, citation, and synthesis so answer quality depended on evidence quality as well as wording.
  • System behavior Strong approaches used enough retrieval depth for coverage, structured answers around facets, and kept claims close to cited sources.
  • Takeaways Durable lessons include careful evidence selection, citation discipline, and answer planning; setup-specific tactics need fresh validation.

Transition

What Changed From 2025 To 2026?

The 2026 track keeps the core retrieval-plus-synthesis challenge but changes the evidence collection and citation target through ClimbMix.

What stays useful

Careful evidence selection, citation discipline, and answer planning remain useful from 2025.

What changes

ClimbMix introduces a broader 2026 evidence collection and makes official document IDs the citation anchor for submitted evidence.

Risk areas

A 2025-style pipeline can under-retrieve, overfit old topic patterns, or lose traceability if chunk IDs replace official document IDs.

Strategy

How I Would Approach 2026

A practical build plan can move from baseline retrieval to structured evidence selection, answer drafting, citation checking, and controlled iteration.

Start with baselines

Build a transparent pipeline first so retrieval misses and citation failures are easy to diagnose.

Instrument evidence

Track which passages were retrieved, selected, cited, ignored, or contradicted by the final answer.

Iterate with rubrics

Use the evaluation criteria to drive experiments instead of chasing isolated leaderboard movement.

Evaluation

How To Think About Grading

The evaluation section should help readers distinguish answer quality, evidence quality, and the connection between the two.

Grounding

Every important claim should be traceable to the cited evidence, not merely plausible.

Completeness

A high-quality answer covers the user need without adding unsupported extras or skipping key facets.

Usefulness

Readable structure, calibrated confidence, and concise synthesis make evidence easier to act on.
