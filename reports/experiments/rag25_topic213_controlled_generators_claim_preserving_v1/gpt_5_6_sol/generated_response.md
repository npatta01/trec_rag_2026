## What motivated US involvement in the Korean War?

North Korea's invasion of South Korea on June 25, 1950, directly triggered the involvement of the United States military. [shard_02825_37436]

## What impact did the Korean War have on US politics?

The Korean War produced a significant increase in military spending, contributing to the development of a powerful military-industrial complex in the United States. [shard_01057_25693]

President Truman's dismissal of General Douglas MacArthur triggered a political firestorm and seriously damaged Truman's approval ratings, contributing to the end of his presidency. [shard_06013_13419]

The Korean War intensified public fears of communist subversion, prompting investigations by the House Un-American Activities Committee and fostering belief in widespread communist infiltration of American institutions. [shard_03477_77362]

## How did the Korean War conclude?

The Korean War concluded with the signing of an armistice on July 27, 1953. [shard_00436_61907]

The armistice that ended the Korean War was a truce rather than a peace treaty, so the conflict remains technically ongoing. [shard_00839_47882]

Because no formal peace treaty was ever signed to end the Korean War, the conflict remains officially unresolved and technically ongoing. [shard_03461_23898]

Fighting in the Korean War ended in a stalemate, without a clear winner or loser. [shard_01355_13797]

The armistice restored the boundary along the 38th parallel and established the Demilitarized Zone, a fortified buffer set at 2.5 miles wide. [shard_01259_11973]

## How does the Korean War affect UN

On June 25, 1950, the United Nations Security Council passed Resolution 82, authorizing member states to provide military assistance to South Korea in response to North Korea's invasion. [shard_02701_45655]

The United States led a United Nations coalition during the Korean War, and American forces supplied most of the coalition's combat power. [shard_01408_73258]

The Korean War ended with an armistice signed on July 27, 1953, by North Korea, South Korea, China, and the United Nations, but no formal peace treaty was ever ratified. [shard_05971_76579]

## What motivated China involvement in te Korean War?

China entered the Korean War to support North Korea against United Nations forces that were led by the United States. [shard_00627_41410]

China feared that United Nations advances, particularly toward the Yalu River, would cause encirclement or regime change in North Korea, prompting Chinese intervention. [shard_02011_43116]

Mao Zedong argued that allowing the United States to occupy all of Korea would weaken communist revolutionary power in Asia and permit American influence to spread negatively throughout the Far East. [shard_01400_83904]

China's initial objective in the Korean War was not to conquer South Korea but to preserve North Korea as a buffer state. [shard_05252_10180]
