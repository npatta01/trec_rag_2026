## Extraction method

The model received source-exact sentence/paragraph chunks and returned only
integer label and passage identifiers. Final strings were copied from the
source documents by identifier, not generated by the model.

Five mixed-topic documents over 80,000 characters were bounded to
Korean-War-keyword-matched source segments, adjacent context, and at most 300
segments before their single model call. The other 168 documents supplied all
prepared source chunks.

Adjacent selected fragments were merged only when the intervening source
characters were whitespace. Every resulting span was then checked as an exact
Unicode substring of the JSON-decoded source text. No whitespace normalization,
ellipses, edits, or offsets were used.
