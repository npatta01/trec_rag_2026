Despite the 1953 armistice, the Korean War remains a negotiated truce to this day and has never officially ended. [shard_06013_13419; shard_03461_23898]

## How does the Korean War affect Korea

The Korean War began in 1950 when North Korea invaded South Korea under orders issued by Kim Il Sung. [shard_06031_32421; shard_05351_5394]

A truce signed in 1953 left Korea divided near the 38th parallel, without a peace treaty ratified by the belligerents. [shard_03682_83132; shard_05958_47285; shard_03484_77279]

The July 1953 armistice established the Demilitarized Zone between North and South Korea, which remains among the world’s most heavily fortified borders. [shard_03423_69502; shard_02854_6140]

The United States suffered 33,686 casualties during the conflict, while South Korea suffered more than two million casualties. [shard_05215_66447]

The Korean War created a four-kilometer-wide buffer called the Korean Demilitarized Zone, which is demarcated by the Military Demarcation Line. [shard_05971_76579; shard_05264_15774; shard_00276_43322]

## How does the Korean War affect UN
